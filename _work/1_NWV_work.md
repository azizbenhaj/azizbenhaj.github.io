I work as an **AI/ML Engineer at New Wind Ventures**, a Swiss investment intelligence company focused on African markets. The company leverages artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data engineering to transform complex economic, political, governance, and sector-specific data into actionable insights, helping investors identify opportunities, assess risks, and make informed investment decisions across Africa.

## My role as an AI/ML Engineer:

As an AI/ML Engineer at New Wind Ventures in Geneva, I am: 
- developing and integrating AI-driven solutions and data pipelines to transform complex, fragmented data into actionable investment intelligence.
- building machine learning models, automating data processing workflows, and developing analytical tools that assess macroeconomic, political, governance, and sector-specific indicators to identify investment opportunities and evaluate risks across African markets.
- contributing to integrating these capabilities into interactive dashboards and data-driven platforms that support investment decision-making.
